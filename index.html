Linga Bhairavi Stuti The 33 Names of the Divine Feminine

An exploration of the names, meanings and qualities behind the sacred chant.

Chant the full Linga Bhairavi Stuti from the text below. To understand what you chant, twenty slides explain the meaning of each of the 33 names of Devi, with Sadhguru's words on the Grace of Bhairavi.

The 33 names

Tap a name to open its slide.

The full Stuti

Chant 1, 3, 6, 9 or 11 times. Turn on meanings for a devotee's understanding of each name.

  1. Jai Bhairavi Devi Gurubhyo Namah Shri

    To the Gurus: we bow first to the Guru.

  2. Jai Bhairavi Devi Swayambho Namah Shri

    The self-born one, who exists by herself.

  3. Jai Bhairavi Devi Swa dhārini Namah Shri

    She who holds herself up, and holds all of us.

  4. Jai Bhairavi Devi Mahā Kalyāni Namah Shri

    The great auspicious one, who brings all that is good.

  5. Jai Bhairavi Devi Mahā Bhadrāni Namah Shri

    The great blessed one, who keeps us safe and well.

  6. Jai Bhairavi Devi Maheshwari Namah Shri

    The great Goddess, the great ruler.

  7. Jai Bhairavi Devi Nāgeshwari Namah Shri

    Queen of the serpents.

  8. Jai Bhairavi Devi Vishweshwari Namah Shri

    Ruler of the whole universe.

  9. Jai Bhairavi Devi Someshwari Namah Shri

    Ruler of Soma: the moon and its cool nectar.

  10. Jai Bhairavi Devi Dukha Samhāri Namah Shri

    She who ends sorrow.

  11. Jai Bhairavi Devi Hiranya Garbhini Namah Shri

    She who carries the golden womb, the seed of creation.

  1. Jai Bhairavi Devi Amrita Varshini Namah Shri

    She who showers nectar like rain.

  2. Jai Bhairavi Devi Bhakta Rakshini Namah Shri

    Protector of those who love her.

  3. Jai Bhairavi Devi Saubhāgya Dāyini Namah Shri

    Giver of good fortune and a happy life.

  4. Jai Bhairavi Devi Sarva Janani Namah Shri

    Mother of all.

  5. Jai Bhairavi Devi Garbha Dāyini Namah Shri

    She who blesses the womb with new life.

  6. Jai Bhairavi Devi Shoonya Vāsini Namah Shri

    She who lives in emptiness.

  7. Jai Bhairavi Devi Mahā Nandini Namah Shri

    The great joy; she who fills us with delight.

  8. Jai Bhairavi Devi Vāmeshwari Namah Shri

    Goddess of the left side, the feminine half of life.

  9. Jai Bhairavi Devi Karma Pālini Namah Shri

    She who watches over our karma, our actions and their fruits.

  10. Jai Bhairavi Devi Yonīshwari Namah Shri

    Goddess of the womb, the source of all life.

  11. Jai Bhairavi Devi Linga Roopini Namah Shri

    She who takes the form of a linga.

  1. Jai Bhairavi Devi Shyāma Sundari Namah Shri

    The dark, beautiful one.

  2. Jai Bhairavi Devi Trinetrini Namah Shri

    The three-eyed one.

  3. Jai Bhairavi Devi Sarva Mangali Namah Shri

    Auspicious in every way; she makes all things good.

  4. Jai Bhairavi Devi Mahā Yogini Namah Shri

    The great yogini, one with all of life.

  5. Jai Bhairavi Devi Klesha Nāshini Namah Shri

    She who destroys our troubles and afflictions.

  6. Jai Bhairavi Devi Ugra Roopini Namah Shri

    She whose form is fierce and mighty.

  7. Jai Bhairavi Devi Divya Kāmini Namah Shri

    The divine beloved.

  8. Jai Bhairavi Devi Kāla Roopini Namah Shri

    She whose form is Time itself.

  9. Jai Bhairavi Devi Trishoola Dhārini Namah Shri

    She who holds the trident.

  10. Jai Bhairavi Devi Yaksha Kāmini Namah Shri

    Beloved even of the Yakshas, the unseen celestial beings.

  11. Jai Bhairavi Devi Mukti Dāyini Namah Shri

    Giver of liberation.
